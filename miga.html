Microglia Genomic Atlas

How do inherited variants shape gene expression and RNA splicing in human microglia?

My contribution

I helped lead MiGA, a collaborative atlas of freshly isolated human microglia. I applied expression quantitative trait locus (eQTL) mapping and colocalization to connect genetic regulation in microglia with variants implicated in brain disorders.

What we found

We mapped genetic effects on gene expression and splicing in human microglia. Comparing these maps with genetic associations for brain disorders helped prioritize candidate genes and regulatory mechanisms for follow-up.
