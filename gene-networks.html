Gene networks and neuropathology

Which gene expression programs in specific brain cell types track with Alzheimer’s disease traits?

My contribution

I led a study using single-nucleus RNA sequencing from the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex of participants in the Religious Orders Study and Rush Memory and Aging Project (ROSMAP). We built co-expression networks to identify groups of genes that covary and examined how those groups relate to disease traits.

What we found

Many gene modules had similar co-expression patterns across cell types. Some showed cell-type-specific differences in how genes were connected. Comparing the networks with neuropathological traits helped identify gene programs for follow-up.
